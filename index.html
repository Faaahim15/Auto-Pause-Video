🎬 How It Works

1. Watch a Video

Start watching your favorite video on YouTube or any supported site.

2. Switch Tabs or Apps

When you switch to another tab or app, the extension automatically pauses the video so you never miss a moment.

3. Return & Resume

When you return to the video tab, playback resumes automatically—seamless and effortless!
